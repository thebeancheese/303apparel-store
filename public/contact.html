Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, sizing and returns. Can't find yours? Send us a message above.

Ordering & payment

How do I place an order?

Open any product, pick your size and quantity, then tap Add to cart or Order now. At checkout, enter your name, email, mobile number and delivery address, choose a payment method and place your order. You'll get an order number right away.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery and GCash. If you choose GCash, our team will contact you with payment details before your order ships.

How do I use a promo code?

Open your cart, type the code in the promo box and tap Apply. For example, 15FF takes 15% off while the promo is running. The discount shows in your total before you check out.

Shipping & delivery

How long does delivery take?

Angeles City and Pampanga: 2–4 days. Rest of Luzon: 3–7 days. Visayas and Mindanao: 5–10 days. Delivery times start once your order is confirmed.

How can I check on my order?

Keep your order number from checkout and send it to us through the contact form or our social pages. We'll update you on where your order is.

Sizing & returns

How do I find my size?

Every product has a Size guide under the size picker with measurements for that type of piece (tops, outerwear, sets or kids). If you're between two sizes, size up for a relaxed fit.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. You have 7 days after delivery to return or exchange unworn items with tags still attached. Send us your order number through the contact form and we'll sort it out.
